# Synthetic Vacation Policy — Version 1.0

**Policy key:** `synthetic-vacation`
**Effective from:** 2025-01-01
**Status:** active

An employee may request vacation up to the available balance. Requests that
exceed the available balance, overlap a leave period, or approach a contract
end date require People Operations review before approval. Approval is not an
automatic employment action: an authorized reviewer records the final
decision.

This document is fictitious and exists only for the PeopleOps AI demo.
