# Synthetic Payroll Procedure — Version 1.0

**Policy key:** `synthetic-payroll`
**Effective from:** 2025-01-01
**Status:** active

Payroll explanations must identify the comparison periods, changed concepts,
and supporting attendance or overtime facts where available. A discrepancy
between recorded and paid overtime is an exception for review; the copilot
must not modify payroll or make a disciplinary recommendation.
